WSET PrepStation privacy policy

This policy covers the WSET PrepStation iPhone and iPad app and these product/support pages provided by CraigTEC Studio. The current app version is an offline study tool. It has no separate app account, AI review service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated study-data server.

1. Information saved on your device

The app saves your language and appearance preferences, first-run setup state, study plan, answers, sessions, results, learning history, favorites, review information and tasting notes on your device. Reminder choices are also stored locally. These records are not uploaded to CraigTEC Studio.

2. Local notifications

Study reminders use scheduled local notifications on the weekdays and time you select. Notification permission is optional. You can turn reminders off in Me → Study reminder or change permission in iOS Settings. We do not operate a remote push-notification service for this app.

3. Purchases and Apple services

Optional lifetime content purchases, transaction verification and restoration use Apple’s App Store services. We do not receive your full payment-card information. Apple handles Apple Account, payment and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. No separate WSET PrepStation account is required; an Apple Account may be required for purchases or restoration.

4. Optional email support

If you choose to contact craigtec.studio@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you send. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Your email provider and our email provider process the correspondence under their own policies. The app does not automatically attach study records to an email. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up or applicable obligations. You may request deletion by email; where information must be retained, we will explain the reason and applicable limits.

5. Website and external links

These pages have no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies or local-storage code. Loading a page still sends ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to the website hosting provider, GitHub Pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an external website or sending email uses the service you choose and its privacy policy.

6. Data sharing and tracking

We do not sell your personal data, track you across other apps or websites, or send your answers to an AI provider in this version. Study records remain on the device. Apple services, website hosting and email support operate as described above. We may disclose support information if required by applicable law.

7. Deletion, settings and backups

In the app, choose Me → Delete local progress and confirm to remove saved study records, including exam sessions, history, tasting notes and study-plan records. Language, appearance, first-run setup and reminder settings are stored separately and are not reset by this action. Turn reminders off separately if you no longer want them.

Removing the app and managing device or iCloud backups are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings. Deleting records in the app or removing the app does not necessarily delete existing system backups. Review your Apple backup settings if you also want to manage those copies. We cannot restore records that were stored only on your device.

8. Updates and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change and show the new effective date here. For questions or requests about this policy, email craigtec.studio@gmail.com.

WSET PrepStation 隱私政策

本政策適用於 CraigTEC Studio 提供的 WSET PrepStation iPhone／iPad App，以及本產品與支援網頁。目前版本為離線學習工具，不另建 App 帳號、不提供 AI 批改，也不使用廣告 SDK、分析 SDK 或由開發者營運的學習資料伺服器。

1. 裝置上的資料

App 會在你的裝置保存語言與外觀偏好、初次設定狀態、備考計畫、答案、作答場次、結果、學習歷史、收藏、複習資訊與品飲筆記。提醒設定亦保存在本機。這些紀錄不會上傳給 CraigTEC Studio。

2. 本機通知

讀書提醒依你選擇的星期與時間，以本機通知排程。通知權限可自行決定；可在「我的 → 讀書提醒」關閉提醒，或在 iOS 設定中調整權限。我們不為本 App 營運遠端推播服務。

3. 購買與 Apple 服務

可選的終身內容購買、交易驗證與恢復購買，使用 Apple 的 App Store 服務。我們不會取得完整付款卡資料。Apple 依其條款與隱私政策處理 Apple 帳號、付款及交易資訊。學習不需另建 WSET PrepStation 帳號；購買或恢復購買可能需要 Apple 帳號。

4. 自行選擇的 email 支援

若你主動寄信至 craigtec.studio@gmail.com，我們會收到你的 email 地址，以及你寄送的訊息或附件，並用於回覆請求及調查問題。你的郵件服務商及我們的郵件服務商會依各自政策處理往來信件。App 不會自動將學習紀錄附在 email 中，請避免寄送敏感個人資訊。

支援信件僅在處理該請求、相關後續或履行適用義務所需期間保留。你可透過 email 要求刪除；如有必須保留的資料，我們會說明原因與適用限制。

5. 網站與外部連結

本網頁沒有廣告、分析腳本、追蹤像素、Cookie 或 localStorage 程式。載入頁面時，網站託管服務 GitHub Pages 仍會接收 IP 位址等一般連線資訊，請參閱 GitHub 隱私聲明。開啟外部網站或寄送 email 時，適用你選用服務的隱私政策。

6. 資料分享與追蹤

我們不出售個人資料、不跨 App 或網站追蹤你，本版亦不把答案傳送給 AI 服務商。學習紀錄保存在裝置上。Apple 服務、網站託管與 email 支援的處理方式如上述說明；在適用法律要求時，我們可能依法提供支援往來資料。

7. 刪除、設定與備份

在 App 選擇「我的 → 刪除本機學習紀錄」並確認，可移除保存的學習資料，包括作答場次、學習歷史、品飲筆記與備考計畫紀錄。語言、外觀、初次設定與提醒設定另行保存，不會由這項操作重設。如不再需要提醒，請另外關閉。

移除 App 及管理裝置／iCloud 備份，由 iOS 與你的 Apple 設定控制。在 App 刪除紀錄或移除 App，不一定會刪除既有的系統備份；若也要管理備份副本，請檢查 Apple 備份設定。我們無法恢復只保存在你裝置上的紀錄。

8. 更新與聯絡方式

資料處理方式如有變更，我們會更新本政策與生效日期。對本政策有疑問或需提出請求，請寄信至 craigtec.studio@gmail.com。
