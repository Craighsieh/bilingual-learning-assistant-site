Support

Contact craigtec.studio@gmail.com for app issues, content corrections, purchase questions or privacy requests.

Please include your app version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. For a content correction, include the question or lesson identifier and language if available. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending them. Never send passwords, verification codes or full payment-card details.

Purchases and restoration

Use the app’s restore-purchases control with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple manages payment and transaction records. Contact Apple for billing or refund requests. We cannot retrieve study records that exist only on your device.

Local progress and reminders

To delete saved study records, open Me → Delete local progress and confirm. Language, appearance and reminder settings are stored separately. Turn reminders off in Me → Study reminder, or manage notification permission in iOS Settings. See the privacy policy for details about local storage and backups.

WSET PrepStation 支援與產品說明
